How to use Ace Models Timer

A walkthrough for moderators: starting and confirming a shift, tracking time, filing the shift report, managing fan CRM data, and using the built-in AI tools.

01 Overview

What the app is for and the main areas you'll use.

Ace Models Timer is the moderator dashboard for tracking cam-model shifts. Each shift moves through the same lifecycle: confirm you're ready → work the shift with the live timer → file a mandatory report when you stop. Alongside that, the toolbar gives you four supporting tools:

Toolbar buttonWhat it's for
Good MembersFan CRM — log habits, triggers, spend tier per member
Shift HistoryEvery past shift, its report, and edit/delete controls
ActivityStudio performance dashboard, CRM insights, coverage suggestions
AskNatural-language search over your full shift + CRM history
●
All data is stored locally on this machine, next to the app. If you moderate on more than one computer, shift history and CRM entries don't sync between them.

02 Starting a shift

Click Start on the main window to open the New Shift dialog.

  1. Pick the Model and Moderator from the dropdowns, or type a new name and use the + button to add it to the list. * edits and − deletes the selected entry.
  2. Optionally add shift notes.
  3. Set a shift duration goal (hours / minutes) — this drives the progress bar on the main window.
  4. Click Start Shift.

03 Pre-shift checklist

Before the timer can start, you confirm you're actually ready.

This window shows:

  • Handoff — the previous completed shift's actual report for this model (not just old notes), so you know what happened last time.
  • Heads Up banner — appears only when AI detects a genuinely recurring issue across recent shift reports or CRM triggers for this model, not a one-off mention.
  • VIP fans — a quick list pulled from CRM records for this model.
  • A 6-item tech checklist (lighting, framing, OBS scene, mic, background, hotkeys) — all six must be checked before Confirm & Start is enabled.
●
The Heads Up banner costs an AI call only when there's real repeated signal — it's skipped automatically most of the time, so seeing it means it's worth reading.

04 During the shift

The main window while a shift is running.

The big clock counts elapsed time (not a countdown), and occasional milestone pop-ups mark progress. The bar underneath fills toward your shift-duration goal. Use Show / Private / Typing to broadcast the model's current status, and Pause if you need to stop the clock without ending the shift.

▲
Closing the app while a shift is active always asks you to confirm first — it won't discard a running shift silently.

05 Ending the shift & the report

Click Stop to end the timer. The shift report window opens immediately and can't be skipped.

  • Session summary is required — how the shift went overall.
  • Good members / VIPs to note and Issues to watch are optional but shape next shift's handoff and Heads Up banner.
  • Model mood / performance — a 1–5 rating.
  • Once you've typed your own summary, an ✨ Polish with AI button appears. It rewrites your notes into a clearer paragraph for the next moderator — it never invents content you didn't write.
✕
The report window won't close until you submit — there's no way to cancel out of it. If you need to fix something after submitting, use Edit from Shift History (see next section).

06 Shift history & editing

Every recorded shift, filterable by date range, model, or moderator.

Click any row's Notes cell to view the full text in a popup. Use Edit to reopen a submitted report and correct or add to it — the form is prefilled with what was originally submitted, including the AI polish option. Delete removes the record entirely.

07 High traffic & CRM

Track fan habits, triggers, and spend tier per model.

  1. Enter a User, pick the Model and Platform, then Save.
  2. Add Habits, Triggers, and free-text Notes — these feed the pre-shift Heads Up check and the handoff VIP list.
  3. Click ✨ Suggest tier & check duplicates to have AI recommend a spend tier from the habits/triggers/notes you entered, and flag if the username looks like a likely duplicate of an existing entry.
  4. Filter the table by platform using the dropdown in the title bar.

08 Activity dashboard

Studio-wide performance, at a glance.

Filter by date range and moderator, then Apply. The dashboard shows daily-average hours, top performer, and days tracked, followed by three CRM insight panels — Top Spenders, Fans to Re-engage, and Most-Worked Days. ✨ Suggest Coverage asks AI to look at hours-by-day-of-week per moderator and point out under-covered days. Export the underlying data with Export CSV or Export Excel.

09 Ask AI

A plain-English search over your full shift and CRM history.

Type a keyword into Search for a quick text match — no AI needed. For something that requires reasoning across records — "What's Katyerave's biggest weakness this month?" — click ✨ Ask AI about the full shift & CRM history. The answer comes back as a headline, supporting bullet points, and a small bar chart when the data actually warrants one.

10 Settings & AI setup

Theme, hotkeys, notifications, and the AI provider.

Every ✨ AI feature across the app (Polish with AI, Ask AI, risk flags, tier suggestions, coverage suggestions) is powered by whatever provider you configure here:

  1. Open Settings from the toolbar.
  2. Choose a provider — Anthropic Claude or OpenRouter (a free-tier option).
  3. Paste your API key into the key field and save.
▲
The API key is currently stored in plain text in the app's local settings file. Don't share this machine's app folder, and treat the key like a password.

11 Troubleshooting

I clicked an ✨ AI button and nothing seems to happen

Look for the button label changing to "Thinking…" — a real answer usually takes a few seconds. If nothing changes at all, open Settings and confirm a provider and API key are set.

The window I closed came back / won't close

The Shift Report window is intentionally mandatory — it won't close until you submit. The main window always asks for confirmation before closing so an active shift isn't lost by accident.

Can two moderators share the same data?

Not yet — shift history and CRM records live on the machine they were entered on. Handoff notes and risk flags only work if the same computer is used shift to shift.

I need to fix a report after submitting it

Open Shift History, find the row, and click Edit. Changes save back to that exact record.
